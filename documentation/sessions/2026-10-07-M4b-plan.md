## Review loop 1 (senior full-stack/desktop-UI persona, research-only)

**Verdict: 6.0/10** — sub-ratings: interaction design 6, state/undo design 5,
predicate/architecture 6, test plan 6, estimate realism 4. 15 findings: 2
blockers, 7 majors, 6 minors. Full critique text is preserved in the task
output; findings summary:

- BLOCKER 1 — template application timing ambiguous (brief said "applied
  corner becomes template"; M4 seam enqueues at Confirm — every downstream
  artifact forks on the reading).
- BLOCKER 2 — planned `similar_corners(primitives, template: Dogbone)`
  signature computes with data that no longer exists: apply_corner removes run
  members post-apply, per-member lengths unrecoverable.
- MAJOR 3 — click semantics self-contradictory (click = toggle AND click =
  zoom-through; deselect interaction never defined in the ADR).
- MAJOR 4 — red/green fails deuteranopia AND collides with the app's own
  red=deletion grammar (theme.py `delete: #ff4d4d`); ADR-018(n) precedent
  already ruled against red/green coding for M7.
- MAJOR 5 — wheel-zoom `Viewer.redraw` does `delete("all")`; dot layer would
  vanish after one scroll notch; no re-render specified.
- MAJOR 6 — canvas bindings double-fire: left-miss propagates to
  fsm.click_world and starts an edge pick inside dot mode; Esc/right-click race
  the mode's exit.
- MAJOR 7 — ApplyQueue.apply_all pushes ONE snapshot per batch; per-corner
  granularity + "apply_panel.py untouched" don't compose as written.
- MAJOR 8 — Undo Batch lifecycle traps (superseded-work destruction; zero-mutation Go; below-pre-Go plain undos; one shared Undo toast for two granularities).
- MAJOR 9 — predicate equivalence classes under-defined (reflection vs
  role-swap never stated; Side.sign is edge1-relative so swap flips the
  bisector; ±2° on half-angle = 4° on corner angle; mapping has no algorithm —
  wrong-side placement accepted by the pipeline).
- MINORS 10–15 — enumeration/dedup undefined ("document order" ambiguous);
  hover compute-on-Motion wasteful (precompute + per-dot deletion counts);
  summary format byte-drift risk; ADR-019(d) "zero pending review" contradicts
  (e) warn-and-skip; DoD gaps (0-match, all-red, zoom survival, interleaved
  undo, deterministic toggle-negative drive, view restore, dot min size);
  estimate ~2x optimistic.

**Actions taken (ADR-020 appended, binding; brief rewritten to match):**
ruling (a) template applies at Confirm as its own one-corner batch, Apply All
