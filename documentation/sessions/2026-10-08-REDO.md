## Done

Redo is a second deep-copy stack on `SnapshotStack` (ADR-027). Undo
pushes the state it leaves onto that stack; redo swaps it back.
`push` (Apply All and trim confirm) and `revert` clear it. Fold
designation and a sticky flag decision are not their own undo steps;
they call `discard_redo` so a later redo cannot overwrite that edit.
The sync path after undo/redo (`on_model_change`) does not clear the
branch it just created.

UI: Redo sits beside Undo in the tool panel. Disabled when the redo
stack is empty.
