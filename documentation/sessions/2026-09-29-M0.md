WORKED-EXAMPLE's 90° dogbone uses half-angle 45° on the *material* side — same number by coincidence, different physical side. The brief's own site-detection predicate + center formula + every printed center in sections 1-9 are internally consistent under the brief's rule, and it was the one the pre-verify script reproduced everywhere — so I followed the brief and flagged the conflict for M4 (no canon doc edited).
2. **syn-L-bracket air side** — brief says air NE / center (31.1225, 31.1225); my derivation (and the brief's own "notch void" rule, § "Angle convention") says air **SW** into the notch void, center (28.8775, 31.1225). The user's call ("Keep file, doc SW center"): use the geometrically-correct SW center, note the supersession in the manifest.
3. **syn-fold-heavy air side** — same defect as #2. Brief said air SW / center (18.8775, 58.8775), but that point is **inside material** (winding check); the void of the step is NW. Corrected to NW, center (18.8775, 61.1225); fold distances re-derived (18.8775 / 23.872 / 46.2301 / 31.1228 / 44.0139 mm).
4. **syn-arc-tears ARC A2** — brief writes center (96.25, 53.8775), span 180→360, min-dist-to-c8 "0.1275 < R ⇒ chord-crosser". Analytic min distance of that ARC to c8 is 2.6225 (the stated 0.1275 is actually d(arc_CENTER, c8), a category error). As written the ARC never got within 0.1275 of c8, so the intended FLAG case does not occur. All other ARC/LINE claims (A1 endpoint-in, L1 chord-crosser 0.2974, L2 endpoint-in 1.3047) verified exactly as written and left untouched.
5. **syn-multicorner winding** — brief's original CCW vertex list wound CW on the reflex sites (cross > 0 at slot-floor corners ≠ the stated predicate). Swapped to a top-edge-CW-reading list (`(200,100)→(180,100)→(180,80)→…`) which is the same outline and restores the stated site rule and all four documented centers. Adjacent-centre gaps re-checked: 17.755, 22.245, 17.755 (all > 2R); census is 13 LINEs + 2 CIRCLEs (the 16-vertex figure was a different draft; the 13-edge version satisfies "three 20×20 slots + multicorner chaining").

7. **`neg-r12.dxf` could not be produced by `ezdxf.new("R12")` + `doc.header["$INSUNITS"] = 4`** — ezdxf explicitly refuses to emit a DXF R12 drawing-units group ("Drawing units ($INSUNITS) are not exported for DXF R12").
