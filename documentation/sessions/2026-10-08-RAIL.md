# 2026-10-08 — Rail column scroll and trim hint

User requests, not a milestone. The left rail already had collapsible sections and a pinned Undo/Redo/Revert strip (session log `documentation/sessions/2026-10-08-UI-SECTIONS.md`).

## What changed

The Trim section shows a muted line under **Trim to Closest**: "Click a line near the end you want to trim or extend. Ctrl+Z undoes." The string is `TRIM_HINT` in `ui/messages.py`.

Dogbone, Folds, and Trim pack into one column in `Sidebar.body`. They scroll together, and only when that column is taller than the rail. When the sections already fit, the wheel leaves them at the top. The view also clamps so the column cannot shift down into blank space above Dogbone. Undo, Redo, and Revert stay on `Sidebar.pin` and do not scroll.

The current layout is recorded on `documentation/briefs/UI-UPGRADE.md` ("Revision — 2026-10-08") and on ISSUE-021.
